Key rules

No armor, ever. Wearing armor disables Unarmored Defense, Martial Arts die, and Dexterous Attacks simultaneously.
Adrenaline Rush before Step of the Wind. Both Dash as Bonus Action — but Rush recharges on Short Rest and adds Temp HP. Save FP for when you also need Disengage.
Short Rest often. FP recharge on Short Rest. Uncanny Metabolism is backup when you can't rest.
Monk weapons only. Simple melee or Light martial only. Two-handed and Heavy weapons break Martial Arts.
Bonus Unarmed Strike is always free. After the Attack action (with Unarmed Strike or Monk weapon), one Unarmed Strike as Bonus Action costs nothing.
