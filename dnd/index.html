Fight flow

When combat starts Long Rest · 1×
Uncanny Metabolism. On your initiative roll: regain ALL Focus + heal 1d6 + 3 Monk lvl 3 + 1d6.
Assess & position. Note threats and cover, then use your 40 ft 30 base + 10 unarmored to set up a good first turn.
Your action
Attack roll. d20 + 5 DEX 3 + PB 2 vs the target's AC. On a hit, roll damage.
Damage. 1d6 + 3 Martial Arts 1d6 + DEX 3 bludgeoning per Unarmed Strike.
Grapple replaces an attack STR/DEX save vs DC 13 8 + DEX 3 + PB 2. On a fail — Speed 0, drag it as you move.
Shove replaces an attack STR/DEX save vs DC 13 8 + DEX 3 + PB 2. On a fail — push 5 ft or knock it prone.
Stabilize a downed ally. full action DC 10 WIS (Medicine) check on a creature at 0 HP; your Medicine is +6 — WIS 4 + PB 2. Any healing also revives it.
Bonus Action — pick ONE
You get one Bonus Action per turn. The 0-FP Unarmed Strike is one of these options, not a freebie stacked on top.
Unarmed Strike free one extra Unarmed Strike (Martial Arts).
Flurry of Blows 1 FP two Unarmed Strikes.
Patient Defense free Disengage (or +Dodge for 1 FP).
Step of the Wind free Dash (or +Disengage for 1 FP).
Adrenaline Rush Short Rest · 2× Dash + 2 Temp HP.
When Attacked one reaction / round
Deflect Attacks. When hit by bludgeoning/piercing/ slashing damage, reduce it by 1d10 + 6 DEX 3 + Monk lvl 3. If reduced to 0, spend 1 FP to redirect: DEX save DC 14 8 + WIS 4 + PB 2 or take 2d6 + 3 DEX 3.
Opportunity Attack. When a creature you can see leaves your reach, one melee attack against it. (Disengage via Patient Defense or Step of the Wind avoids giving enemies theirs.)
Dropping to 0 HP Relentless · Long Rest · 1×
Relentless Endurance first. If you'd hit 0 HP and haven't used it this long rest, drop to 1 HP instead.
Otherwise, death saves. Roll d20 at the start of each of your turns: 10+ succeeds, 9 or less fails. Nat 1 = 2 failures; nat 20 = wake with 1 HP. Taking damage at 0 HP = 1 failure (2 on a crit).

Key rules

No armor, ever. Wearing armor disables Unarmored Defense, Martial Arts die, and Dexterous Attacks simultaneously.
Adrenaline Rush before Step of the Wind. Both Dash as Bonus Action — but Rush recharges on Short Rest and adds Temp HP. Save FP for when you also need Disengage.
Short Rest often. FP recharge on Short Rest. Uncanny Metabolism is backup when you can't rest.
Monk weapons only. Simple melee or Light martial only. Two-handed and Heavy weapons break Martial Arts.
Bonus Unarmed Strike costs 0 FP — but it's your Bonus Action. Martial Arts lets you make one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action with no Focus. It still uses your single Bonus Action, so it competes with Flurry, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind, and Adrenaline Rush.
