# Working guidance

## Communication

- When a task is done, don't reiterate what was done if it can be figured out
  from the context (the conversation, the diff, the ledger). Only surface what
  isn't otherwise discoverable — surprises, decisions, caveats, or what's left.

## Level-up modifiers (maintenance note for `index.html`)

When Stick levels, these numbers change — update them in Quick Stats, the feat
cards, and the Fight flow section wherever they appear:

- **Focus Points** = Monk level (now 3) — drives the Bonus Action pick-ONE FP
  costs and the Deflect/redirect line.
- **Martial Arts die** grows with level (1d6 now → 1d8 at lvl 5) — the Quick
  Stats damage line and the "Your action" Damage figure (`1d6 + 3`).
- **Deflect & Uncanny** scale with Monk level: reduce `1d10 + DEX + level`, heal
  `1d6 + level`.
- **PB-driven DCs** (feature DC 14, Grapple/Shove DC 13, Medicine +6) all rise
  when Proficiency Bonus does (next at lvl 5). The attack roll `d20 + 5` (DEX 3 +
  PB 2) and initiative `d20 + 3` move too.
- **Unarmored Movement** speed bonus increases at lvl 6+ — the 40 ft on the "When
  combat starts" card.
